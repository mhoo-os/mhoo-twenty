Privacy Policy

How Mhoo handles personal data across its website, Workspaces, and customer-connected business systems.

Effective August 3, 2026 · Updated August 14, 2026

1. Who we are

MHOO Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") is a Thai technology company and the operator of Mhoo. We act as controller for this website and our own business relationships. When we process information for a customer inside that customer’s Workspace, the customer may be the controller and MHOO Co., Ltd. may act as its processor or service provider.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Mhoo, we may collect:

  • contact and account information, including name, business email, phone number, employer, and role;
  • authentication, Workspace membership, permission, and security-event information;
  • messages, files, photos, business records, and instructions that you or an authorized customer connects to Mhoo;
  • integration data from services such as LINE, Google, Meta products, CRM, point-of-sale, or other systems selected by the customer;
  • technical data such as IP address, device, browser, request identifiers, and reliability or abuse-prevention logs; and
  • communications with MHOO Co., Ltd., including support, sales, consent, and deletion requests.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure Mhoo; authenticate users; enforce Workspace permissions; retrieve and present authorized business knowledge; operate customer-requested workflows; communicate with users; maintain records and receipts; prevent fraud and abuse; comply with law; and improve reliability. We do not use customer Workspace content to advertise to third parties.

4. AI and automated processing

Mhoo may use machine-learning models to search, classify, summarize, draft, or assist with work. Model output is not treated as the original business record. Where a material external action is supported, Mhoo is designed to require the applicable authority and an exact human confirmation before the action is committed.

5. Legal bases

Where a legal basis is required, we process data to perform a contract, follow your or our customer’s instructions, pursue legitimate interests such as security and service reliability, comply with legal obligations, or act with consent. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis, without affecting earlier lawful processing.

6. Sharing and processors

We share information only as needed with authorized customer users, service providers that host or support Mhoo, connected platforms selected by the customer, professional advisers, and authorities when legally required. Providers may include infrastructure, database, storage, communications, identity, monitoring, and model services. We require providers to handle data under appropriate contractual and security obligations.

7. International transfers

MHOO Co., Ltd. is established in Thailand and serves businesses in the United States. Data may be processed in Thailand, the United States, or other locations where our contracted providers operate. We use appropriate contractual, organizational, and technical safeguards when data crosses borders.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, satisfy the customer’s instructions, maintain security and effect receipts, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Deleting one conversation does not automatically delete independent business records, source evidence, financial records, or records another controller must retain. To request deletion, email who@mhoooo.com from the connected address when possible. Do not send passwords, tokens, identification, or sensitive content by email.

9. Security

We use access controls, Workspace isolation, encryption where appropriate, audit and effect receipts, bounded service credentials, and operational recovery practices. No system is perfectly secure. Please contact us promptly if you believe an account or integration has been compromised.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act and relevant United States state laws, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection; withdraw consent; or appeal a privacy decision. We may need to verify your identity and determine whether MHOO Co., Ltd. or a customer is responsible for the request. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

11. Children

Mhoo is a business service and is not directed to children under 18. Do not submit a child’s personal information unless an authorized business has a lawful operational reason and appropriate permission.

12. Contact and changes

Questions or requests may be sent to who@mhoooo.com or MHOO Co., Ltd., 48/49 Soi Ramkamhaeng 102, Saphan Sung, Bangkok 10240, Thailand. We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date.
